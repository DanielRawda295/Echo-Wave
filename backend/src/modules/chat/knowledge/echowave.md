# EchoWave app guide

EchoWave is a music web app with:
- Discover / Top Charts / Top Artists (iTunes-based browsing)
- Song playback with a global player (preview URLs)
- Favorites
- Playlists (create, rename, reorder, remove items)
- Community uploads (upload an mp3 + optional cover and publish)
- Community engagement (likes + comments on uploaded tracks)

## Playing music
- Click the play button on a song card to start playback in the bottom player.
- The player supports iTunes previews and community uploaded tracks.

## Favorites
- Tap the heart icon on any song card to add/remove it from Favorites.
- Favorites persist for your user in the app.

## Playlists
- Create a playlist from the Playlists page.
- Add songs to a playlist using the “Add to playlist” action.
- In playlist details you can:
  - Play all / shuffle play
  - Rename the playlist
  - Reorder songs (up/down)
  - Remove songs

## Uploading songs (Community)
- Go to Upload to publish a song from your device.
- Required: title, artist, genre, and an mp3 file.
- Optional: cover image.
- Uploaded songs appear in the Community feed and can be played like other tracks.

## Account
- You can update username/email/password and avatar.
- You can logout.
- You can deactivate your account (requires password confirmation).
